# Supplementary Videos

## Overview

This folder contains five supplementary videos associated with manuscript Figures 3b, 3d, 4a, 4c, and 5d. The videos show representative imaging of retinal ganglion cells (RGCs) following the onset of breathing.

The ExRai-AMPKAR videos show paired 800 nm and 920 nm excitation images, 920/800 excitation-ratio pseudocolor maps, and intensity heatmaps across Z-planes. The pHLemon-rev FRET video shows donor and acceptor fluorescence images, FRET pseudocolor maps, and the corresponding ΔFRET time course.

Each video is stored in a separate folder labeled by manuscript figure and experimental condition. The MP4 filenames use the figure identifiers; the legends identify the corresponding Supplementary Videos 3–7.

## Video legends

### Supplementary Video 3

**Manuscript figure:** Fig. 3b. **Video file:** `Fig.3b.mp4`.

The video shows representative <em>in vivo</em> time-lapse imaging corresponding to <strong>Fig. 3b</strong>, in which AMPK-F activity in neonatal RGCs of <em>Vglut2-Cre</em> mice was monitored using the ExRai-AMPKAR biosensor following breathing onset. Images acquired at 800 nm and 920 nm excitation wavelengths, together with the corresponding 920/800-nm ratio pseudocolor images, are shown at the indicated time points. Z-stack views illustrate the RGCs monitored across imaging planes, with the plane containing the maximal signal highlighted for individual cells. The ExRai-AMPKAR 920/800 nm excitation ratio progressively increased following breathing onset, indicating the activation of AMPK-F signaling in RGCs after breathing onset.

### Supplementary Video 4

**Manuscript figure:** Fig. 3d. **Video file:** `Fig.3d.mp4`.

The video shows representative <em>in vivo</em> time-lapse imaging corresponding to <strong>Fig. 3d,</strong> in which AMPK-F activity in neonatal RGCs of <em>Vglut2-Cre;</em> <em>Snrk<sup>fl/fl</sup></em> mice was monitored using the ExRai-AMPKAR biosensor following breathing onset. Images acquired at 800 nm and 920 nm excitation wavelengths, together with the corresponding 920/800-nm ratio pseudocolor images, are shown at the indicated time points. Z-stack views illustrate the RGCs monitored across imaging planes, with the plane containing the maximal signal highlighted for individual cells. Loss of SNRK largely abolished the initial rise and markedly attenuated the later increase in AMPK-F activity following breathing onset, indicating that SNRK is a major mediator of respiration-triggered AMPK-F activation in neonatal RGCs.

### Supplementary Video 5

**Manuscript figure:** Fig. 4a. **Video file:** `Fig.4a.mp4`.

The video shows representative <em>in vivo</em> time-lapse imaging corresponding to <strong>Fig. 4a</strong>, in which AMPK-F activity in neonatal RGCs of <em>Vglut2-Cre</em>  mice delivered in 10% CO<sub>2</sub> was monitored using the ExRai-AMPKAR biosensor following breathing onset. Images acquired at 800 nm and 920 nm excitation wavelengths, together with the corresponding 920/800-nm ratio pseudocolor images, are shown at the indicated time points. Z-stack views illustrate the RGCs monitored across imaging planes, with the plane containing the maximal signal highlighted for individual cells. Elevated CO₂ exposure largely abolished the respiration-triggered increase in AMPK-F activity in neonatal RGCs, indicating that CO₂ clearance is critical for AMPK-F activation at birth.

### Supplementary Video 6

**Manuscript figure:** Fig. 4c. **Video file:** `Fig.4c.mp4`.

The video shows representative <em>in vivo</em> time-lapse imaging corresponding to <strong>Fig. 4c</strong>, in which AMPK-F activity in neonatal RGCs of <em>Vglut2-Cre</em>  mice delivered in 10% O<sub>2</sub> was monitored using the ExRai-AMPKAR biosensor following breathing onset. Images acquired at 800 nm and 920 nm excitation wavelengths, together with the corresponding 920/800-nm ratio pseudocolor images, are shown at the indicated time points. Z-stack views illustrate the RGCs monitored across imaging planes, with the plane containing the maximal signal highlighted for individual cells. Elevated O<sub>2</sub> exposure did not attenuate the postnatal increase in AMPK-F activity.

### Supplementary Video 7

**Manuscript figure:** Fig. 5d. **Video file:** `Fig.5d.mp4`.

The video shows intracellular pH dynamics in retinal ganglion cells (RGCs) following the onset of breathing, monitored using the pHLemon-rev FRET biosensor, associated with manuscript <strong>Fig. 5d.</strong> Donor and acceptor fluorescence images, FRET pseudocolor maps, and the corresponding ΔFRET time course are displayed. The progressive increase in FRET signal reflects intracellular alkalinization following breathing onset.
